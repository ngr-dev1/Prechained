What verification proves
📅 Pre-incident proof
The receipt timestamp proves the record existed before any specified incident window. Cryptographically immutable.
⬡ Bitcoin anchored
Records are anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain. Independently verifiable at blockstream.info without trusting Prechained.
🔐 SHA-384 integrity
Every snapshot is SHA-384 fingerprinted. The fingerprint proves the package state at capture time was not altered.
⚖️ Legally defensible
Bitcoin-anchored timestamps constitute evidence of prior state under ESIGN and eIDAS. Shareable with auditors and legal teams.
